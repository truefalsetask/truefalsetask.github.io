Support

Questions, bugs, ideas: write to truefalsetask@gmail.com. I read every message.

It helps if you add:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the app version;
  • what you did and what happened. A screenshot or a short screen recording says a lot.

Common questions

Pro is missing after reinstalling the app or on a new iPhone.
Open the Pro screen and tap «Restore Purchases». Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
In the app's settings, tap «Create Full Backup» and save the file to Files (iCloud Drive is the easiest). On the new iPhone, open the same settings and tap «Restore from Backup».

I'm testing through TestFlight. How do I report a problem?
Take a screenshot in the app: TestFlight offers to send it to me with a comment. Or just write to the address above.

What happens to my data?
It stays on your iPhone. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
