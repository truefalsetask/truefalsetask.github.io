Privacy Policy

True-False Task (T-F Task) · Last updated: 8 November 2026

T-F Task is made by German Borodin, an independent developer based in France. In short: what you put into the app stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

Your tasks, habits, focus sessions, counters, countdowns, shopping lists, finances, nutrition log, attached photos and settings are stored on your device, in the app and its widgets. I can't see any of it.

  • Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) may include this data, depending on your settings.
  • A backup you create in the app is saved to the Files app, wherever you choose.
  • You can erase everything in the app's settings, or by deleting the app.

When the app goes online

Only for the features below, and only when you use them.

  • Product lookup. When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the barcode or your search text goes to Open Food Facts, a free food database. It sends back the product's name, ingredients, labels, photos and prices. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • Product recalls. The barcode of a scanned product goes to RappelConso, the French government's product recall service (data.economie.gouv.fr), to check whether the product has been recalled.
  • Product pictures. When you pick a picture for a product, its name goes to Openverse and Wikimedia Commons, which search openly licensed images.

Each request tells the service the app's name, version and contact address and, like any connection, your IP address. Nothing else goes with it: no name, no device identifier, no location.

Links to a web search or a shop's website open in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

Apple features

The app asks before it uses any of these, and you can change your answer at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

  • Health: reads steps and active energy for the monthly summary in Nutrition, and writes the calories, protein, fat and carbohydrates of what you mark as eaten. The app never sends Health data anywhere and never uses it for advertising.
  • Calendar: shows your Apple Calendar events next to your tasks and countdowns.
  • Reminders: lets you move your lists and tasks into T-F Task. Nothing is changed in Reminders.
  • Camera: scans barcodes and takes photos for tasks and habits. The photos stay on your iPhone.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: turn your dictation into the text of a task or habit. Apple may process the recording on its servers; see Apple's privacy policy.
  • Notifications and alarms are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Purchases: T-F Task Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never see your payment details. The app only learns whether Pro is active.

Your rights

I don't collect personal data, so I have nothing about you to show, correct or delete. To remove the app's data, erase it in the app's settings or delete the app. For data sent to Open Food Facts, RappelConso, Openverse or Wikimedia, contact them directly.

For questions or complaints, write to truefalsetask@gmail.com. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority, at cnil.fr.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
